COUNT DEFINITIONS

New complaints: reported within the selected Nairobi interval, through the
captured cutoff for an ongoing period.

New complaints resolved: the intersection of those newly reported complaints
with distinct complaints having a resolution in the same interval.

Older complaints resolved: reported before the interval, with a resolution
inside it. Unclassifiable reporting dates form an honest Other count rather
than being invented as an older or same-period complaint.

Reopened: a complaint with an in-period resolution that is currently open again.
It retains its resolution activity and also belongs in the current open backlog.
Repeated closure events count the complaint once in the period total.

Open overall: all currently unresolved complaints in the existing authorized,
non-date-filtered report scope. The overdue number is a subset of this backlog.
It is not new complaints minus all resolutions, or reconstructed period-end
status. The data-as-of stamp identifies when that current state was captured.

Example: 6 new complaints were reported in October 2026. 2 of these were
resolved in the same period, along with 3 older complaints. 5 complaints remain
open overall, including 4 overdue.

A complaint reported in October and resolved on 1 November is not an October
resolution. Older stored snapshots without the new aggregate intersections use
explicitly labelled totals, never a guessed same-period breakdown.

CHARTS

Summary: Reported vs resolved line/marker trend + on-time/late doughnut.
Turnaround performance: resolution-time trend + first-response-time trend.
Open-case follow-up: chronological age bars + response-coverage doughnut.

The default follows the information: trend lines/markers, composition rings,
ordered distribution bars. One temporal interval uses grouped bars because
there is no trend to join. Missing points stay missing; a measured zero duration
is valid. Zero-denominator compositions show a plain empty state, not NaN or 0%.

Reported uses blue, resolved/on-time green, late/no response red, caution amber.
Cartesian axes are dark and thicker than the lighter grid. Units are explicit;
count ticks are whole numbers. Doughnuts show counts and percentages and support
one full-category result. Graph text is larger and legends are compact.

PNG rendering remains offline through the existing Pillow dependency. Logo and
graphs remain inline CID images. Plain text and Excel retain complete datasets;
unreadable/failed graphs retain a bounded fallback, never lose the report file.
There are no app links, remote assets, interactive email tabs or new dependencies.
